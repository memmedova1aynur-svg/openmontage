# Audio guide
- Music stretched to exactly 120 BPM (1 beat = 0.5 s = 15 frames); scene changes land on beats. Timeline: `src/productions/<id>.timeline.json`.
- Voice chain + per-sentence levelling, music EQ-carved (2.5 kHz) and sidechain-ducked, loudnorm -14.5 LUFS, TP -1.5.
- SFX: real recordings (Mixkit), placed by timeline events with peak alignment. Re-check Mixkit licences before publishing.
